## The product, in one breath
**CallFlag.** An AI listens to a bank's hardship calls and raises a live danger sign carrying the
reply-due date and one question to ask next. Then a report card. Then the calls that went wrong become
de-identified practice customers. Track 3, plus the ElevenLabs track.

## The demo, as the team designed it
Run the same call **twice**. Coaching off: the staff member misses the notice, ends on "I'll put a note
on the file", and the report card shows the miss. Coaching on: the sign fires at 45 seconds, the staff
member names the hardship process, the report is clean. **The gap between the two runs is the
argument.** A judge does not have to believe a claim they can watch.

## Rules
- **Never claim novelty without searching.** We once wrote "no bank has done this" and a search found
  Bendigo and Adelaide Bank already detecting hardship after the call. The honest claim is **live,
  during the call, with the deadline and the next question** - which is stronger anyway.
- **Never state a number we have not measured**, and never imply a feature works when it does not. If
  anonymous sign-ins are still off, do not show an account flow.
- **Cite the law precisely**: National Credit Code s72 and its 21 days, ASIC RG 271 and its 30 days,
  the ABA financial difficulty guideline. Get the section numbers right or leave them out.
